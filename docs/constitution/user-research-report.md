# Aveyra — User Research Report (validation, July 2026)

The empirical basis for the [communication-first positioning](volume-2-product-strategy.md).
Validates the concept before further development.

**Sample: 13 participants** (small; skewed young). Age: 18–24 ×9, 25–34 ×1,
35–44 ×1, <18 ×1, n/s ×1. Status: dating ×8, single ×3, married ×2.
**Validated target:** young adults 18–24, especially dating couples.
*(Caveat: small, skewed sample — Volume 2 strategy §9 calls for expanding to
50–100 with more married/long-distance/25–45 before the production release.)*

## Findings
1. **Feeling understood matters — 76.9%** say being deeply understood is
   important (53.8% very). Validates the core purpose: help two people understand
   each other, not just store memories.
2. **Meaningful conversations are rare** — 38.5% "rarely," only 23.1% daily.
   Validates **Daily Questions as the primary feature**.
3. **Emotional distance is common — 92.3%** have felt they don't know someone
   close as well as they'd like. Aveyra should surface thoughts/feelings people
   don't normally discuss.

## Biggest relationship challenges
Lack of communication **84.6%** · busy schedules 69.2% · distance 61.5% ·
difficulty expressing feelings 53.8% · forgetting moments 38.5% · life changes
38.5% · repeating conversations 15.4% · less intimacy 7.7%.
→ **The core problem is intentional communication, not love.**

## How people preserve memories today
Photos/videos 61.5% · messages/chats 30.8% · physical keepsakes 23.1% · social
posts 15.4% · don't preserve 15.4% · journal/notes 7.7%.
→ Memories are **fragmented across apps**. Aveyra organizes them into a
**relationship story**, not another photo store.

## Interest & concerns
- Interest: **definitely 46.2% + maybe 38.5%** (≈85% positive). Caveat: must
  clearly explain **why it's different from messaging apps**.
- Top concerns: **privacy, security**, feeling unnatural/forced, too much effort,
  unclear value vs normal communication.
- What makes conversation meaningful: honesty · listening · understanding the
  other's perspective · sharing beliefs · emotional openness · peace afterwards ·
  mutual interest · remembering small details. → should shape Daily Question design.

## Feature validation (build-priority signal)
- **Strongly validated:** Daily Questions · Guided Conversations · Shared Memory
  Timeline · Partner Connection · Privacy & Security.
- **Moderately validated:** Private Journal · Relationship Reflections · Gentle
  Notifications.

## Product implications (must)
Prioritize privacy · communicate security clearly · never make conversation feel
forced · minimize effort · demonstrate value quickly.

## Conclusion & North Star
People aren't after another messaging app or photo gallery — they want a **simple,
trustworthy way to communicate more intentionally, understand each other better,
and preserve the moments that matter.**
> **Every interaction in Aveyra should help two people know each other a little
> better than they did yesterday.**
