# Love Maps — Example Questions (author-provided, 2026-07-22)

Reference content for the M2 question bank. "Love Maps" is a **sub-category under
Discovery** (Section E). These are in Aveyra's tone — gentle, curious, concrete —
and should seed / calibrate the Discovery category when the bank is re-mapped to
the 9 canonical categories (open-questions Q14). Voice and depth here are the
target for the whole bank.

## Everyday life & small details
- What's a tiny everyday thing that makes your day feel better, even if no one else notices?
- If I could quietly help with one small task in your week, what would you secretly love it to be?
- When you get home after a long day, what's your ideal first fifteen minutes?
- Which part of your morning routine feels most "you"?
- If you had a free hour with no obligations, how would you most likely spend it?

## Preferences & comforts
- What's a simple comfort you wish I remembered more often (a snack, a song, a gesture)?
- Is there a place you go — real or imagined — when you need to feel calmer inside?
- What kind of surroundings make you feel most at ease (light, sound, people, quiet)?
- Which type of message from me (short text, long note, call, voice note) feels most supportive?
- What's a small thing I do that you'd happily keep as part of our "forever" routine?

## People, history & inner world
- Who are two people (past or present) who have quietly shaped who you are today?
- When you think about your childhood, what's one scene you hope I can picture clearly?
- What's a moment in your past that still influences the way you see relationships?
- Is there a story about you that you wish I asked about more often?
- What's one value your family taught you that you still carry, even if the context changed?

## Stress, support & emotional landscape
- When life feels heavy, what kind of response from me usually helps — listening, problem-solving, distraction, or something else?
- What are a few early signs that you're stressed, even before you say it out loud?
- When you feel misunderstood, what's one thing I could do to make repair easier?
- What helps you feel safe enough to be fully honest with me, even about hard things?
- What's a small reassurance you'd love to hear from me more often?

## Dreams, hopes & identity
- What's a quiet dream you hold that you haven't talked much about yet?
- When you imagine "a good year" for yourself, what does it actually look like day to day?
- What kind of person are you hoping to become in the next few years?
- What would make you feel proud of us when you look back ten years from now?
- What's one thing you hope I'll always remember about who you are, even if circumstances change?
