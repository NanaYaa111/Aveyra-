# Positive Love — Research Extract

Isolates healthy, secure, *positive* love (separated from toxic/obsessive
material) and grades it, so the product builds only on what holds up.
**Grades:** A replicated/longitudinal · B single studies / contested ·
C practitioner consensus · D weak or contradicted (never load-bearing).

## 1. What positive love *is* — Grade A
Love is a configuration of **feelings + cognitions + behaviours** sustained over
time. Two consequences: (1) love is partly **behavioural**, therefore partly
practisable — a product can *occasion behaviour* (its entire licence to exist);
(2) **intensity ≠ health** — obsessive love is love mixed with anxiety/control.
**Never optimise for emotional intensity.**

## 2. Theories, graded
- **Sternberg's Triangular Theory — B (most usable).** Intimacy (**servable
  directly** via disclosure), Commitment (**servable indirectly** via the
  accumulating shared record), Passion (**not an app's domain**). *Intimacy +
  commitment are the two live targets; anything serving neither is out of scope.*
- **Attachment — A dimensional, D as typing.** Insight usable (secure love =
  closeness *without engulfment*; autonomy is health, not a deficit). **Do not
  build an attachment-style quiz.**
- **Lee's love styles / Greek forms — C as vocabulary, D as taxonomy.** Same
  category error as love languages. Use as *craft language* ("this prompt is in
  the ludus register"), never to classify users or filter content.

## 3. Forms of positive love — product relevance
- **Agape (act-based love) — the biggest gap.** Everything in Phase 1 is verbal
  disclosure; agape is expressed in *acts*. **Add prompts that produce an action,
  where the entry is simply that it happened.** Cheap, high-value.
- **Philautia (self-compassion) — best independent evidence.** Converges with
  expressive-writing. **The solo journal (Write) is a first-class, evidence-backed
  feature — framed as legitimately the user's own,** not a placeholder.
- **Ludus (play) — real component.** The question bank needs **tonal range:
  mostly light, occasional depth.** Not all vulnerable prompts.
- Eros/passion out of scope (correctly). Philia/storge precluded by the
  two-partner assumption (but see §6 participants question).

## 4. Markers of healthy love (operational, Grade C)
Each person remains whole · boundaries clear & respected · jealousy addressed by
conversation not control · conflict = listening/repair/responsibility · a steady
presence not a constant emergency · reciprocity · closeness without engulfment ·
emotional regulation. **Four of eight are about *restraint*** — the product is
defined as much by what it declines to do as what it offers.

## 5. How love develops — Grade C
Attraction → deepening → commitment/integration → maintenance/transformation.
**Aveyra's value curve targets the maintenance stage** (where most of a
relationship's duration sits, least market attention, and the archive is worth
most). **Onboarding must not assume a new relationship.**

## 6. Wellbeing — Grade B (and the participants question)
Loving relationships → better health markers, lower depression/anxiety, more
resilience. **Critically, these effects appear in familial and close-friendship
love, not only romantic love.** → the strongest argument for widening the
participant model (**`participants`, not `partner`, in the schema**). *Logged as
an open decision for Milestone 2 — a real fork from the two-romantic-partners
framing, not adopted silently.*

## 7. Convergence with the Phase 2 dossier (all Grade A/B)
Bids for connection (raise the noticing rate on small moments) · 5:1 ratio (default
output = positive interaction volume) · **Perceived partner responsiveness** (the
mechanism most good features work through — design test: *does this make one person
more likely to feel understood?*) · Gratitude · Expressive writing.

**The gratitude boundary condition (most important caveat):** benefits accrue only
where the partner is perceived as responsive; injected gratitude in a
low-responsiveness relationship can backfire. Gratitude must be **specific
("name a thing they did"), optional, never obligatory, never streak-linked.**

## 7.5 Generational material (Grade C/D) — three additions, one conflict
- **Positive love is *active*, not merely non-toxic.** The product needs
  something it *offers*, not only harms it avoids. ("Emotional safety" ≈ PPR.)
- **Shared purpose — a genuine structural gap.** Aveyra *records*; it doesn't let
  two people *build* something forward-facing. **One** forward-looking shared
  object may be worth more than many retrospective ones — **scoped as one thing,
  a later phase** (not the cut 60-feature bucket-list category).
- **Boundary-supporting design** (reduce mediation): user-chosen notification
  windows, "quiet days," and **nothing anywhere that rewards frequency of use.**
- **Community accountability — cannot serve.** The sealed two-person, no-server
  architecture excludes third parties by design. **Accept the trade-off
  explicitly: Aveyra chooses privacy over community accountability.**

## 8. What to refuse, on the evidence
Any **typology** (love languages / love styles / attachment types — same error);
any **relationship score**; any **detection of unhealthy patterns**; **passion
features**; **anything obligatory**.

## 9. Gaps neither source addresses (state openly)
Cultural range (Western/Greek framing only); same-sex & non-monogamous
relationships (unvalidated by the cited frameworks); long-distance; what sustains
love over *decades* (the exact stage Aveyra targets); sustained **play** as a
health component.

## Decisions this research drives (for Milestone 2)
| Adopt now (build) | Defer / decide |
|---|---|
| Question bank with **tonal range** (mostly light) | `participants` vs `partner` schema — **open decision** |
| **Agape act-prompts** (entry = it happened) | One forward-facing **shared object** — later phase |
| **Write** framed as first-class (philautia) | Boundary-supporting settings (quiet days) — later |
| Gratitude **specific + optional**, never obligatory | Community accountability — **won't serve** (documented) |
| Onboarding that **doesn't assume a new relationship** | Cultural/relationship-structure breadth — note openly |
