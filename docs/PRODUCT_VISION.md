# Product Vision — Ours

## Mission
Help couples stay close by making the small, healthy habits of a good
relationship — remembering, appreciating, communicating, planning — easy,
warm and private.

## Vision
A relationship platform people genuinely love because it strengthens
communication, preserves memories and helps couples grow together — without
ever mining their most intimate data.

## Target users
- **Committed couples** (dating, engaged, married) who want a private shared
  space that isn't a social feed.
- **Long-distance couples** who need connection rituals and countdowns.
- **Privacy-conscious people** who refuse to put their relationship on someone
  else's server.
- **Busy couples** who want gentle nudges to appreciate each other, not
  another attention-harvesting app.

## Problems solved
1. **Scattered memories** — photos, notes and dates live across a dozen apps.
   Ours keeps them in one warm timeline.
2. **Communication drift** — couples run out of things to *really* talk about.
   The Connection Deck restarts meaningful conversation on demand.
3. **Forgotten moments** — missed anniversaries and un-said thank-yous.
   Countdowns and notes fix that.
4. **Privacy anxiety** — mainstream couple apps monetise intimacy. Ours stores
   nothing off-device.
5. **Unhealthy engagement** — most apps optimise for time-on-screen. Ours
   optimises for *a stronger relationship*, then gets out of the way.

## Unique value proposition
> The only couples app that is **premium, deeply useful, and completely
> private by architecture** — it works offline, keeps everything on your
> device, and is built on relationship science rather than dark patterns.

## Competitive advantages
- **Local-first & zero-tracking** — a structural guarantee, not a policy promise.
- **Evidence-based content** — Gottman, attachment theory, love languages,
  gratitude science — not generic prompts.
- **Healthy gamification** — streaks that celebrate, never punish; no
  manipulative loops.
- **Premium craft** — Apple/Notion/Linear-grade polish with its own warm identity.
- **No dependencies, instant, durable** — fast, auditable, and future-proof.

## Success metrics (health-aligned)
We deliberately avoid engagement-maximising metrics. We measure:
- **Meaningful actions per week** (memories saved, notes written, cards drawn,
  check-ins completed) — value delivered, not minutes trapped.
- **Streak retention without pressure** — do couples come back willingly?
- **Backups created** — a proxy for "this data matters to me."
- **Qualitative** — "did this help you feel closer?" in-app pulse.
- **North star:** *Weekly Connected Couples* — couples who took at least one
  meaningful, mutual action in the last 7 days.
