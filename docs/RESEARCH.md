# Research

Research that informed the product. We studied competitors and the
relationship-science literature, then deliberately built something different
rather than copying features.

## Competitive landscape

| Product | Loved for | Common complaints | What Ours does differently |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| **Between** | Private shared timeline, clean chat | Ads/upsells, data on their servers, feature bloat | Same intimacy, but local-only and ad-free |
| **Paired** | Daily questions grounded in research | Hard paywall, requires both partners subscribed | Evidence-based deck, free and offline |
| **Lasting / OurRitual** | Structured therapy-style sessions | Expensive, clinical, subscription | Lightweight, self-paced, no account |
| **Agapé** | AI relationship coaching | Sends intimate data to the cloud/LLM | Privacy-first; any future AI stays opt-in/on-device |
| **Lovewick / Official** | Gamified challenges & streaks | Gimmicky, manipulative streak pressure | Streaks that celebrate, never punish |
| **Love Nudge** | Built on the 5 Love Languages | Dated UX, narrow scope | Love languages as one thread in a premium whole |
| **Gottman Card Decks** | Excellent question content | Standalone, no memory/journal features | Gottman-style deck *integrated* with the rest |
| **SumOne / Noteit / Widgetable** | Delightful widgets & prompts | Thin, ad-supported, cloud-bound | Depth + delight without the data cost |
| **Cozy / Evergreen** | Warm design, milestones | Sync-dependent, paywalled backups | Warm design + free local backups |

**Recurring gaps we exploit:** privacy is an afterthought everywhere; content
is often generic; monetisation frequently relies on dark patterns; few apps
are genuinely useful *offline*.

## Relationship psychology (evidence base)

- **Gottman Method** — *Love Maps* (knowing each other's inner world),
  *Fondness & Admiration* (positive sentiment override), *Turning Toward*
  bids, and gentle *Repair*. These directly shape the Connection Deck
  categories.
- **Attachment theory** — secure connection is built through responsiveness
  and reliable rituals; hence gentle daily check-ins and countdowns rather
  than high-pressure demands.
- **Five Love Languages** — captured in profile fields and note types; future
  personalization can tailor suggestions to each partner's language.
- **Gratitude research** — regularly naming appreciation predicts
  satisfaction; the check-in's gratitude journal operationalises this.
- **Positive reinforcement & habit formation** — a light cue → action →
  celebration loop (daily prompt → check-in → warm confirmation) builds a
  sustainable habit without coercion.
- **Long-distance maintenance** — shared countdowns, letters and future
  letters give distant couples anchors and anticipation.

## Behavioral design stance
We follow *intrinsic* motivation: the app is rewarding because the actions
themselves strengthen the relationship. We explicitly avoid infinite feeds,
guilt-based notifications, loss-framed streaks, and variable-reward slot
mechanics. The streak resets quietly and never scolds; celebrations are warm,
not addictive.

## UI/UX references
Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Material Design for interaction and
accessibility baselines; Notion, Linear and Stripe for restraint and craft;
emotional-design principles (warmth, calm, softness) for the couples context.
Color choices follow color psychology — warm rose for affection, gold for
celebration, calm mauve for depth — avoiding childish pink.
